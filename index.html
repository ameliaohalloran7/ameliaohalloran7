I am currently a Pre-Doctoral Research Fellow at the Project on Human Development in Chicago Neighborhoods (PHDCN+) in the Sociology Department at Harvard University.

My work uses primarily quantitative methods and focuses on neighborhood trust, urbanization, domestic violence, incarceration, residential mobility, and deviance.
